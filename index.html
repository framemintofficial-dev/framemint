FAQ

Common
Questions.

Everything you need to know before working with us.

How long does a website project take?

+

Typically 5–10 working days for a standard landing page, and 2–3 weeks for a full multi-page website. Timelines depend on complexity and how quickly you provide content. We always share a timeline before starting.

Do you offer revisions?

+

Yes! Website projects include unlimited revisions. Thumbnails include 2 revisions and video editing includes 3 rounds. We're not done until you're completely happy with the result.

What's your payment process?

+

We take a 50% advance before starting, and the remaining 50% upon final delivery. For monthly packages, payment is collected at the start of each month. We accept UPI, bank transfer and international payments.

Can I get a custom package?

+

Absolutely. Most of our clients opt for custom bundles — for example, combining website design with monthly social media management. Just describe your needs in the contact form and we'll create a custom quote.

Do you work with international clients?

+

Yes, we work with clients globally. All communication is handled in English via email, WhatsApp or video call. We're experienced in working across time zones with creators and brands from US, UK, UAE and beyond.
